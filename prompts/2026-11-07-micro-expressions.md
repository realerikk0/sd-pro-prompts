- Prefer prompts that name concrete facial beats (blink timing, wink windows, restrained/ironic smile, eye contact after a line) over vague “emotional” adjectives.
- For Seedance multi-shot ads or H3 segmented clips, lock face identity in a reference image and only change wardrobe/lighting/camera between cuts.
