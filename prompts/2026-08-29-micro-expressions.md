## Usage Tips (carry-over from recent high-quality examples)

- **Eye-led performance first**: Prioritize gaze direction, slow blinks, brief looks away, and residual expression after the final line. Keep large facial morphs minimal.
- **Timing & lags**: Insert deliberate pauses (0.5–0.8 s). Eyes move before head; mouth and eye muscles should not change simultaneously.
- **Restrained body**: Limit physical action to ~10–20%. Let dialogue + micro-expression carry the emotion.
- **Character lock**: Always reference an image and explicitly forbid face redesign, age drift, AI-influencer face, or identity change.
- **Camera**: Prefer fixed or extremely slow push/pull so micro facial changes remain readable.
- **Emotion curve**: Describe a clear progression (e.g., fatigue → soft self-mockery → quiet resolve → release) rather than a single label like “sad” or “happy”.
