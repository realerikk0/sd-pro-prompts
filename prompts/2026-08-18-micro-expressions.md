## Usage Tips

- Name specific facial muscles (orbicularis oculi, mentalis, corrugator supercilii, procerus, zygomaticus major, levator palpebrae) when possible — this appears to help Seedance 2.5 produce more anatomically plausible micro-movements.
- Sequence: trigger → physiological pause → eye/brow change → mouth/breath residual → line delivery with residual emotion.
- Keep camera movement minimal or one continuous slow push-in so micro-expressions remain readable.
- For surprise: start with a freeze/pause frame of recognition before the expression unfolds.
- Dialogue lines that crack, whisper, or contain micro-pauses improve realism of lip and facial sync.
