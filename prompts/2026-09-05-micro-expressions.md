## 1. Five Micro-Expression Control Methods + Example Prompts (Siri)

**Source**: [@Sirilee2026](https://x.com/Sirilee2026) (AI求道者Siri)  
**Model**: General (works with 即梦 / Kling / Doubao / Qwen; principles apply to Seedance 2.5 & MiniMax H3)  
**Original post URL**: https://x.com/Sirilee2026/status/2095895665809490043  
**Date**: 2026-09-04

**Key insight from author**: AI 漫剧人物为什么像假人？因为你没有控制微表情. Do not dump "sad / cry / laugh" labels. Break performance into sequence, amplitude, body support, and physiological details.

### Method 1: Emotion Threshold Control (情绪阈值控制法)

Write degree adverbs + concrete facial muscle actions instead of max-emotion labels.

**Example prompt (English)**:
```
Extreme close-up shot of a handsome Chinese man in his 30s, East Asian facial features, subtle micro-expressions, tears welling up in his eyes but refusing to fall, slight trembling of the lower lip, skin texture detail, pores visible, eyes glistening with unshed tears, cinematic lighting, rim light, shallow depth of field, 4k, highly detailed, slow emotional build up over 5 seconds, Canon C500 film grain.
```

**Example prompt (Chinese)**:
```
30多岁英俊中国男性的极致特写，东亚人面部特征，微表情，泪水在眼眶中打转但强忍着不落下，下唇轻微颤抖，皮肤纹理细节，毛孔可见，眼睛闪烁着未落下的泪光，电影布光，轮廓光，浅景深，4k，高度细节化，5秒内情绪缓慢积累，最后泪水还是落下了，佳能C500胶片颗粒感。
```

### Method 2: Causal Body-Linkage (因果肢体连动法)

Emotion needs a physical support (hands, shoulders, objects). Body moves first, face follows.

**Example prompt (English)**:
```
Extreme close-up of a young Chinese child (boy/girl) caught doing something wrong, micro-expression sequence: 0-1s sneaky smile while reaching for candy, 1-2s sudden freeze with eyes wide in shock, 2-3s gaze drops down and left with guilty frown, 3-4s lower lip trembles and protrudes, eyes welling up, 4-5s tears forming, mouth curls into crying shape, natural home lighting, shallow depth of field, skin texture, 5 seconds emotional arc, photorealistic, 4k.
```

**Example prompt (Chinese)**:
```
中国幼童（男孩/女孩）做坏事被发现的极致特写，微表情序列：0-1秒窃笑伸手够糖，1-2秒突然僵住眼睛震惊睁大，2-3秒眼神下移左飘内疚皱眉，3-4秒下唇颤抖前伸眼眶泛红，4-5秒眼泪即将落下嘴巴瘪成哭状，自然家居光线，浅景深，皮肤纹理，5秒情绪弧线，照片级真实，4k。
```

### Method 3: Temporal Evolution (时序演变法)

Write clear start → turning point → end. One emotional arc only.

**Example prompt (English)**:
```
Extreme close-up of a young Chinese woman/girl looking at loved one with adoration, micro-expressions of love: 0-1s soft gaze, pupils slowly dilating, 1-2s genuine smile forming, corners of eyes crinkle (crow's feet), 2-3s eyebrows slightly lift, eyes brighten, 3-4s head tilts a little, lower lip pouts gently, 4-5s smile deepens, eyes become crescent-shaped, full of affection, warm golden hour light, soft focus background, 5 seconds, romantic cinematic style, 8k, hyper-realistic, shot on Leica M.
```

**Example prompt (Chinese)**:
```
年轻中国女性/女孩对爱慕对象深情凝视的极致特写，爱意微表情：0-1秒温柔凝视，瞳孔逐渐放大，1-2秒真心微笑浮现，眼角皱起（鱼尾纹），2-3秒眉毛轻微上扬，眼神明亮，3-4秒头微倾，下唇轻噘，4-5秒笑容加深，眼睛弯成月牙，充满爱意，温暖黄金时刻光线，背景柔焦，5秒，浪漫电影风格，8k，超写实，徕卡M拍摄。
```

### Method 4: Visual Focus Guidance (视觉焦点引导法)

Avoid dead "looking at camera". Give eyes a concrete target and describe shifts.

**Example prompt (English)**:
```
Extreme close-up of a Chinese Peking Opera actress (Hua Dan) on stage, elaborate traditional costume and headpiece, eyes expressing deep emotion, micro-expressions: eyes slowly moving from right to left with slight eyebrow raise, subtle smile forming at corners of mouth, teary and sparkling eyes, delicate makeup, soft stage lighting, shallow depth of field, 5 seconds of graceful eye movement, cinematic, 8k, hyper-realistic, shot on Arri Alexa.
```

**Example prompt (Chinese)**:
```
中国京剧花旦演员的极致特写，舞台上，精美传统戏服与头饰，眼神流露深情，微表情：眼珠缓慢从右向左移动伴随眉毛轻微上扬，嘴角浮现含蓄微笑，眼中含泪闪烁，精致妆容，柔和的舞台光，浅景深，5秒优雅眼波流转，电影质感，8k，超写实，阿莱Alexa拍摄。
```

### Method 5: Physiological Breathing Injection (生理呼吸注入法)

Add shoulder/chest rise, natural blinks, swallow, micro-sweat to break the "statue" look.

**Example prompt (English)**:
```
Extreme close-up of a Chinese person (young adult) showing nervousness after lying, micro-expressions of guilt: 0-1s forced smile with slight lip twitch, 1-2s quick glance down-left then back, 2-3s visible swallow (Adam's apple movement), nostrils flare subtly, sweat forming on forehead, 3-4s hand moves to touch nose, eyes dart away, 4-5s lips press then part, breathing quickens, anxious eyes, interrogation room lighting, harsh shadows, 5 seconds, 4k, realistic skin texture, shallow depth of field.
```

**Example prompt (Chinese)**:
```
中国人（年轻成人）撒谎后紧张不安的极致特写，内疚微表情：0-1秒强笑但嘴角抽动，1-2秒快速向左下瞥一眼后收回，2-3秒明显吞咽（喉结动），鼻翼微张，额头出汗，3-4秒抬手摸鼻子，眼神闪躲，4-5秒嘴唇抿紧又松开，呼吸加快，焦虑眼神，审讯室灯光，硬阴影，5秒，4k，真实皮肤纹理，浅景深。
```

## Usage Tips

- Prefer **degree + muscle action** language ("slight trembling of the lower lip", "tears welling but refusing to fall") over emotion nouns.
- Always write a short **time-sequenced arc** (0–1s / 1–2s …) even for 5-second clips.
- Keep **eyes first, head later**; body support (hand to nose, candy reach, head tilt) makes the face feel alive.
- Inject **physiology**: swallow, nostril flare, micro-sweat, breathing change, natural blink cadence.
- For Seedance 2.5 specifically, combine the above with identity lock to reference image + fixed camera + single restrained tear when pure visual performance is desired.
- For MiniMax H3, the same principles improve consistency; the Magnific examples show how explicit "no morphing / consistent character design" helps lock micro-expressions across longer clips.
