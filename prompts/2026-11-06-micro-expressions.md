## Usage tips

- Prefer prompts that name concrete facial beats (blink timing, eye contact after a prop move, restrained smile) over vague “emotional” adjectives.
- For Seedance multi-shot ads, lock face identity in a reference image and only change wardrobe/lighting between cuts.
- When a post is bilingual, keep both language blocks; micro-acting notes often appear only in one language section.
