## Usage Tips

- Continue prioritizing **eyes-first, head-follows** timing, single restrained tear sequences, light physical contact (e.g., chin lift), fixed-camera continuous takes, and identity lock to reference images.
- Explicitly forbid exaggerated crying, simultaneous bilateral tears, large head motions, and dialogue when pure visual micro-performance is desired.
- Carry-over principles from prior days still apply: deliberate pauses, suppressed emotion language (“克制”, “含蓄”, “若有若无”), soft portrait lighting with clear catchlights, and structural timeline breakdowns for longer clips.
