## Usage tips

- Write micro-expressions as a time-coded sequence (e.g. `[0:00-0:03]`, per-second shot ranges) so the model changes one facial cue at a time instead of morphing.
- Pair each expression beat with a small physical cue (lip press before a laugh, slow blink, head tilt, chin tuck) — entries 1–3 all anchor emotion to concrete facial muscle actions.
- Add global locks for identity consistency and negative constraints against plastic skin / exaggerated cartoon expressions, as in entries 1 and 3.
- Related observation from @KOF97AI ([post](https://x.com/KOF97AI/status/2118657844178248134), already listed on 2026-11-07): MiniMax H3 tends to match micro-expressions to voice emotion more naturally, while Seedance 2.5 may need explicit camera-axis constraints.
