## Usage Tips (carried forward)

- Strong pattern that continues to perform well: detailed staged emotional arcs with precise facial micro-movements (lower lip tremble, tears welling then streaming, silent hysterical laugh through tears, resolute eyes after relief, subtle eye shifts before head movement).
- Prefer a single continuous long take + subtle handheld camera texture so the face carries the entire performance.
- Pair rapid emotion shifts with corresponding smooth lighting changes (front soft → side shadow → backlight silhouette → sudden full blaze → candlelight flicker) to amplify micro-expression readability.
- Keep the character completely silent when possible; convey everything through eyes, mouth, chin, breathing, and small hand actions.
- Lock reference images for character and environment and maintain strict identity consistency.
- Write the *situational trigger* and sequence of micro-reactions (gaze → breath → muscle → residual expression) rather than only emotion labels.
- Time-segment every beat and leave short natural pauses between major facial changes.
