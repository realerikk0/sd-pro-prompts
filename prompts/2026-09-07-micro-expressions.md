## Usage Tips

- Prefer **degree + muscle action** language ("slight trembling of the lower lip", "tears welling but refusing to fall") over emotion nouns.
- Always write a short **time-sequenced arc** (0–1s / 1–2s …) even for short clips.
- Keep **eyes first, head later**; body support (hand to chin/nose, subtle reach, head tilt) makes the face feel alive.
- Inject **physiology**: swallow, nostril flare, micro-sweat, breathing change, natural blink cadence.
- For Seedance 2.5 specifically, combine the above with identity lock to reference image + fixed camera + single restrained tear when pure visual performance is desired.
- For MiniMax H3, explicit "no morphing / consistent character design" language helps lock micro-expressions across longer clips.
- Carry-over principles from prior days still apply: deliberate pauses, suppressed emotion language (“克制”, “含蓄”, “若有若无”), soft portrait lighting with clear catchlights, and structural timeline breakdowns.
