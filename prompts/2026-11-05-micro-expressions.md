**1. Opening – Woman speaks**

Cut to a medium close-up of the man. He looks at her sincerely, speaking calmly with a gentle, thoughtful expression.

MAN: "Somebody kind, loyal, somebody I can laugh with. Somebody that's going to hold me down when times get tough."

MAN: "I want us to be best friends. I want kids, a family, a dog, vacations, all that. And when the kids grow up..."

MAN: "What?"

Cut to the woman's close-up. She gives him a very small, genuine smile and replies softly:

WOMAN: "Nothing. Keep going."

**4. A little nervous laughter**

Cut back to the man. He gives a very short, natural laugh, slightly amused and a little embarrassed.

WOMAN: "Nooo."

The man gently interrupts her with a playful, slightly embarrassed expression.

MAN: "That's my bad."

WOMAN: "Nooo, Ellis, you're not."

**6. Final cinematic shot**

Then smoothly transition to a wider two-shot from slightly farther away, showing both standing together against the beautiful pink and golden evening skyline. They maintain eye contact with a subtle, comfortable smile.

Let the background music gently rise and allow the final moment to breathe before the video ends.

- Maintain the exact identities of both reference characters.
- Every spoken line must have accurate character-specific lip-sync.
- Only the person speaking should move their lips. The listener should show natural reactions.

- Background music must remain soft underneath all dialogue.

- Do not paraphrase, omit, rearrange, or invent dialogue.
- No voiceover, no additional characters, no scene transitions to other locations.
