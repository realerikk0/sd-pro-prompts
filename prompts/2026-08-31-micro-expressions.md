## Usage Tips

- **Eye-led performance + AU codes**: Explicit Action Unit references (AU1, AU4, AU7) help guide eyelid, brow, and gaze micro-shifts. Keep body almost static; emotion lives in eyes, breath, and tiny mouth changes.
- **Hard-cut dialogue structure**: Alternate single-character close-ups / over-shoulder shots with precise hard cuts timed to dialogue ends. This gives clean control over emotional beats.
- **Restrained / suppressed emotion**: Prefer “欲滴未滴”, “强忍”, “眼底掠过一丝”, “气息滞涩” over open crying or shouting. Describe the internal tear rather than the explosion.
- **Character & period lock**: Explicitly forbid face drift, clothing change, modern objects, real-person likeness, and axis breaks (180° rule).
- **Lighting for readability**: Warm side / half-face light + shallow depth of field makes micro-expressions pop without needing large facial morphs.
- Carry-over principles: eyes move before head; deliberate pauses (0.5–1.5 s); dialogue is the skeleton; body action ≤ 10–20%; long continuous takes or tightly controlled hard cuts.
