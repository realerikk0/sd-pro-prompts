## Usage Tips

- **H3 strength observed today:** Even ultra-short prompts (“生成视频，自然，有风”) can yield convincing micro-movements (hair, subtle facial shifts) when the source still is high-quality and photorealistic. For more control, the timed shot-sequence style (Prompt 2) remains highly effective for emotional arcs involving tears, lip tremble, and micro-shifts in the eyes.
- Continue applying the proven techniques from previous days:
  - Eyes move first, head follows with a slight delay.
  - Explicit anti-exaggeration language (克制 / restrained / no sudden tears / no AI beauty face).
  - Identity lock + soft portrait lighting with catchlights.
  - Timeline-structured micro-shifts and physiological details (jaw release, single tear welling, suppressed smile).
- The detailed Seedance 2.5 清冷古风落泪微表情 prompt (2026-09-01) and the H3 everyday-couple restrained-tear prompt (2026-09-08/09) remain excellent references for longer, more granular micro-expression writing.
