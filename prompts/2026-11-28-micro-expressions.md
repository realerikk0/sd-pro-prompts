## Prompt 1 — 伤痕面部特写长镜头 / “我不怕你” (Seedance 2.5)

- **Source**: [@MasonMosen](https://x.com/MasonMosen) (Mason Mosen)
- **Model**: Seedance 2.5
- **Original post URL**: https://x.com/MasonMosen/status/2126135575946420463
- **Posted**: 2026-11-27T02:06:37.000Z
- **Note**: 完整英文提示词发在回复 https://x.com/MasonMosen/status/2126135742615416973 。15 秒一镜到底的面部特写，只靠微表情和呼吸：假笑撑住、瞳孔放大、视线闪一下又拉回、下眼睑发抖、冷汗滑过颧骨，最后不再躲。

~~~
prompt 如下 👇
15s single take, close-up locked on the face. ~25yo East Asian man, short dark hair, small scrapes, dried blood, cloth bandage on nose bridge, worn dark leather jacket, sweat on skin. Shallow DOF, threat blurred in foreground. Split light: cold shadow side (fear), warm side light (forced courage).
0-3s: over-steady stare, fake smirk, jaw clenched, shallow breaths, chin up, neck tense.
3-7s: threat moves; pupils dilate, eyes flick left <0.3s then forced back; smirk collapses; visible swallow.
7-11s: eyes dart, lower lids tremble, brow tightens/relaxes faster and faster, sweat beads, lips pressed white.
11-15s: gaze settles and stops fleeing, face goes slack, a cold sweat drop slides down the cheekbone, long sighing exhale, shoulders drop, eyes reddened.
No screaming, no mechanical blinking, no face warping. Core: the mask vs. the fear under it.
~~~

## Usage tips

- @MasonMosen 的伤痕特写核心是「面具 vs. 面具下面的怕」。0–3 秒的假笑和抬下巴必须先撑住，3–7 秒瞳孔放大、视线左闪不到 0.3 秒再被迫拉回，11–15 秒才允许表情垮掉、冷汗滑过颧骨。不要尖叫，也不要机械眨眼或把脸做崩。
- @PixelAigc 的《大明宫》适合练 MiniMax H3 在 20 秒正反打里保住克制的眉眼神态和口型。皇后先目光轻垂再说「尚缺历练」，皇帝只「眉峰微敛」，不要加成争执或哭戏；走位和空镜是为台词服务的，不要改成大场面调度。
- @ou_zhen599 的《The Fire Kiss Spell》把力量感压在眼睑、眉尾、吞咽和几乎没有的笑意上。符文光和火是氛围，不要写成站起来施法或喷发爆炸；最后只允许嘴角几乎要笑又收回去。
- @superfang119 的《雀斑美妆微距》是极端近景，脸的比例和窗光倒影要全程锁死。唇膏只允许轻轻抿一次再分开检查，眨眼只有一次，不要做成完整化妆教程或大幅度转头。
