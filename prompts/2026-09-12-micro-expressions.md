## Prompt 1: Restrained Emotional Progression – Professional Smile to Suppressed Fatigue (Image-to-Video style)

**Source:** [aiyyo (@aiyyozhoushuai)](https://x.com/aiyyozhoushuai)  
**Model:** Not explicitly named (compatible with Seedance 2.5 / MiniMax H3 image-to-video workflows; focuses on micro-expression & emotional progression)  
**Original post URL:** https://x.com/aiyyozhoushuai/status/2098668775675678809  
**Date:** 2026-09-12 07:03 UTC  
**Notes:** Excellent example of *convergent* (restrained) micro-expression work. Emphasis on subtle muscle tension, eye light fading, nasal wing movement, swallow, and suppressed emotion rather than big acting. Handheld breathing camera + wind interaction add naturalism.

### Full Prompt Text

```
图片1是背景，国风服饰是女主。女生近景，站在图片1场景当中，清风吹过，脸微微侧着，发丝被风吹到脸前，眼神安静，表情没有明显情绪。镜头是手持近景，有很轻的呼吸晃动，像摄影机已经开机但演员还没进入表演。画外传来很轻的一句：“好，开始。”听到这句话后，女生才开始表演。她慢慢把视线转向镜头，先露出一个很甜、很自然的笑，像是在努力让人放心。笑容很干净，嘴角柔和上扬，眼睛也跟着弯起来，前两秒完全看不出难过。风继续吹，几缕头发贴到脸上，她没有整理。笑容保持着，但眼神开始慢慢空掉一点。不是悲伤爆发，而是笑得太久之后，脸部肌肉有一点撑不住。嘴角还在笑，眼睛里的光却慢慢弱下来。最后两秒，她依然看着镜头微笑。只是鼻翼轻轻动了一下，喉咙像咽了一口气，眼眶有一点点湿，但不落泪。嘴角努力维持住甜笑，眼神却露出疲惫。最后她轻轻低一下眼，再抬眼，像把情绪重新压回去。
```

**English translation (for reference, not original):**  
Image 1 is the background; the woman in traditional Chinese-style clothing is the female lead. Medium close-up of the young woman standing in the scene from Image 1. A light breeze blows, her face slightly turned, strands of hair blown across her face, eyes quiet, expression neutral with no obvious emotion. Handheld close-up camera with very light breathing movement, as if the camera is already rolling but the actress has not yet entered performance. A soft off-screen voice says: “Okay, start.” Only after hearing this does she begin to perform. She slowly turns her gaze toward the camera and first shows a sweet, natural smile, as if trying to put someone at ease. The smile is clean, corners of the mouth softly rising, eyes also curving. For the first two seconds there is no sign of sadness. The wind continues; a few strands of hair stick to her face and she does not tidy them. The smile is maintained, but the light in her eyes begins to slowly empty a little. It is not an outburst of grief; rather, after smiling too long the facial muscles start to struggle slightly. The mouth corners are still smiling, yet the light in the eyes gradually weakens. In the final two seconds she is still looking at the camera and smiling. Only her nasal wings move lightly once, her throat seems to swallow a breath, and the eye sockets become a tiny bit moist, but no tears fall. She struggles to keep the sweet smile, yet her eyes reveal fatigue. Finally she lowers her eyes slightly, then raises them again, as if pressing the emotion back down.

## Usage Tips

- Today’s strongest find is the restrained progression style above: professional/sweet smile → subtle muscle fatigue → eye light fade → nasal micro-movement + swallow → suppressed wetness in eyes without actual tears. This type of “convergent” emotional writing continues to produce more natural results than exaggerated crying prompts on both Seedance 2.5 and MiniMax H3.
- Pair with strong identity-locked source images (especially traditional/Chinese-style costumes) and light wind + handheld breathing for extra naturalism.
- Previous high-value references remain useful:
  - Timed multi-stage emotional monologues with lighting shifts (Seedance 2.5).
  - Ultra-minimal prompts on high-quality Midjourney stills for H3 image-to-video (“生成视频，自然，有风”).
  - Explicit anti-exaggeration language + physiological details (jaw release, single tear welling, delayed eye movement).
